# STAR programme

Student support

Are you an offer holder with a disability, a care leaver, or estranged? Join us on the STAR programme for a taster of university life.

![](https://assets.lancashire.ac.uk/image/824122457709/image_71h6oav7dd40h6v5rnkvuk1c2j/-B3840-FWEBP)

The STAR programme is run by our Student Wellbeing Ambassadors. They a host a number of events and activities guiding you through the start of your university journey. This can help to ease you in to student life.

Our Ambassadors are all current students with specialist knowledge and training,. They understand how valuable a friendly face can be, particularly when starting university.

## Residential experience

We host overnight residential experiences for students on our STAR programme. This is a great opportunity to meet other students and explore campus before starting your studies in September. You'll stay in student accommodation and get to know university staff.

We have two overnight residential events taking place in August 2026:

-   Tuesday 4 August - Wednesday 5 August 2026 - [book your place through Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/university-of-lancashire-star-residential-4-5-august-2026-tickets-1991450262343?aff=oddtdtcreator)
    
-   Tuesday 25 August - Wednesday 26 August 2026 - [book your place through Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/university-of-lancashire-star-residential-25-26-august-2026-tickets-1991456478937?aff=oddtdtcreator)
    

During your stay, you will:

-   Stay overnight in student accommodation
    
-   Take part in a campus tour and social activities
    
-   Meet support staff and current students
    
-   Meet other students joining the STAR programme and start making connections early
    
-   Get involved in games, cooking, and craft-based social sessions
    

## Student Wellbeing Ambassador Orientation Support

Throughout August and September, you can also book a one-to-one orientation session with a Student Wellbeing Ambassador. You are welcome to book as many sessions as you need throughout the STAR programme.

 Our ambassadors can help you settle in by:

-   Introducing you to the campus
    
-   Answering your questions
    
-   Helping you prepare for university life
    

[Book a one-to-one orientation session on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/star-student-wellbeing-ambassador-orientation-support-tickets-1992423361910?aff=oddtdtcreator)
