## Student support at university

From asking where to find information to dealing with personal difficulties, we offer you real support whenever you need it.

Our dedicated teams are here to help with every aspect of student life. This could be:

-   Cost of living
    
-   Health and wellbeing
    
-   Disability or dyslexia support
    

Whatever you need help with, there's always somebody to turn to.

Read more

**Where to find our student support teams**

Our student support teams operate across our Preston, Burnley and Westlakes campuses. If you're a current student, visit our [Student Hub](https://msuclanac.sharepoint.com/sites/StudentHub/SitePages/Contact-Us.aspx?OR=Teams-HL&CT=1655986231229&params=eyJBcHBOYW1lIjoiVGVhbXMtRGVza3RvcCIsIkFwcFZlcnNpb24iOiIyNy8yMjA1MDEwMTAwOSJ9) to find out opening hours.

## Keeping you safe on campus

Your safety is our priority. Our Security Team watches over Preston Campus 24/7, 365 days a year. A free night bus also runs from Preston library to PR1 and PR2, making sure you get home safely.
